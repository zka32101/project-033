### 1.2 Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Revenue Model**:
- **Free Tier**: 60% of users, $0 revenue (engagement, future monetization)
- **Individual Pro**: 30% of users, $9.99/month
- **Teams**: 8% of users, $49.99/month (5 users)
- **Enterprise**: 2% of users, custom ($50K-500K annual)

**Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)**:

| Segment | % of Users | Monthly Revenue | Weighted ARPU |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|---|
| Free | 60% | $0 | $0 |
| Individual | 30% | $9.99 × 25% conversion | $0.75 |
| Teams | 8% | $49.99 ÷ 5 users × 40% conv. | $0.40 |
| Enterprise | 2% | $50K annual ÷ 12 ÷ 1000 users | $0.17 |
| **Total ARPU** | **100%** | - | **$1.32/month** |

**Conservative Estimate**: $1.00/month per registered user (accounting for churn, downgrades)

**LTV Calculation** (24-month average):
- Monthly revenue per user: $1.00
- Average lifetime: 24 months (2 years)
- LTV = $1.00 × 24 = $24 per user

**Blended LTV** (by segment):
- Free users: $0 (no revenue)
- Individual converters (25%): $9.99 × 12-18 months = $120-150
- Team users (40%): $249.99 ÷ 5 × 12-24 months = $60-120
- Enterprise (80%+ lifetime): $50K+ average

**Conservative LTV ($24) assumes**:
- 25% individual conversion rate (achievable, target 30%)
- 2-year average lifetime (realistic for education)
- No expansion revenue (upsells, premium features)
- 20% annual churn (healthy for education SaaS)

**Upside LTV Scenarios**:
- Base case ($24): 25% conv, 2 years, 20% churn = 6:1 LTV/CAC
- Bull case ($40): 35% conv, 2.5 years, 15% churn = 10:1 LTV/CAC
- Bear case ($15): 15% conv, 1.5 years, 30% churn = 3.75:1 LTV/CAC

**LTV/CAC Ratio**: 6:1 (using conservative $24 LTV, $4 CAC)
- Excellent SaaS metric (>3:1 is healthy, >5:1 is exceptional)
- Payback period: 4 months ($4 CAC ÷ $1/month ARPU)
- Implies sustainable growth with positive unit economics
