# User Onboarding Strategy

**Executive Summary**: Safy's market launch on Dec 1, 2026 will reach 5M users requiring scalable, multi-language onboarding. This strategy ensures users can effectively begin learning on day one while building long-term engagement and community. The framework prioritizes accessibility, progressive complexity, and support across 8 languages and 4 regions.

## 2. TIER 1: ESSENTIAL ONBOARDING EXPERIENCE

### 2.1 First-Login Interactive Tutorial

**Sequence of Steps** (15-20 minutes total):

**Step 1: Welcome & Language Selection** (2 min)
- "Welcome to Safy" message
- Video: Platform overview (animated, 60 seconds)
- Language selection: 8 flags, native language names
- Confirmation: "You've selected [Language]"
- Navigation: Next button

**Step 2: Learning Preferences** (3 min)
- Question 1: "What's your learning pace?"
  - Options: Slow (10 min/day), Moderate (30 min/day), Fast (1+ hour/day)
  - Explanation: "This helps us recommend courses"
  
- Question 2: "What interests you?"
  - Multiple choice: Technology, Business, Language, Creative, Health, Other
  - Explanation: "We'll recommend relevant courses"
  
- Question 3: "What's your goal?"
  - Options: Career growth, New skill, Hobby, Certification, Other
  - Explanation: "Helps us track progress toward your goals"

- Visualization: "Your profile: [Pace], [Interests], [Goal]"

**Step 3: Navigation Tour** (4 min)
- Interactive: Highlight key UI elements
  - Home dashboard: "See your courses here"
  - Search bar: "Find any course instantly"
  - Profile icon: "Manage your account"
  - Help icon: "Get support anytime"
  - Language selector: "Switch languages here"
- User attempts: Click on each area
- Feedback: "Great! You found [area]"

**Step 4: First Course Selection** (3 min)
- Recommended courses (based on interests/pace): Show 3-5 options
- Visual cards: Course thumbnail, title, duration, rating
- Click to select: "Let's start with [Course]"
- Brief preview: 30-second video clip
- Confirmation: "Ready to begin?"

**Step 5: First Lesson Access** (2 min)
- Video: "How to watch a lesson" (interactive)
- Features highlighted:
  - Play/pause controls
  - Subtitles: All 8 languages available
  - Speed control: 0.5x to 2x
  - Notes icon: Click to add notes
  - Progress bar: See how far through course
  - "Mark complete" button
- User action: "Click play to start your first lesson"

**Step 6: Help & Support Introduction** (1 min)
- "Got questions?" section
- 3 help options:
  - In-app help icon (always available)
  - FAQ by topic (searchable)
  - Chat support (link to support page)
- Confirmation: "You're all set! Enjoy your learning."
- Final button: "Start My First Lesson"

### 2.3 Tier 1 Support Structure

**Help Desk** (Available during launch):
- **Level 1**: Automated chatbot (24/7)
  - Common questions (FAQ)
  - Account access issues
  - Content recommendations
  - Language support: 8 languages
  - Resolution rate: 90%+ of chatbot-only issues
  
- **Level 2**: Support team (24/5, with 24/7 for first week)
  - Real person support (<30 min response)
  - Account issues, payment, technical problems
  - Languages: Japanese, English, Mandarin, Korean primary
  - 8 support agents for launch coverage
  
- **Level 3**: Escalation (business hours)
  - Complex issues, bugs, feature requests
  - Response within 2 hours
  - Languages: Japanese, English
  - 2 senior support specialists

**Escalation Path**:
```
User → Chatbot (resolve 80%)
   ↓
   → Level 2 Support (resolve next 15%)
   ↓
   → Level 3 Escalation (resolve final 5%)
   ↓
   → Product/Engineering team (bug fixes, features)
```
