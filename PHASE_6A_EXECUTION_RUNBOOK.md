#### WEEK 4 (Oct 23-29): Interactive Content Design

**Phase**: Interactive scenarios, simulations, branching decision trees

**Deliverables**:
- [ ] 18 interactive scenarios (1 per module)
- [ ] Visual flowcharts/decision trees
- [ ] Interactive content specifications for development team

**Interactive Content Types** (choose 1-2 per module):

1. **Branching Scenario** (recommended for most)
   - User makes 3-4 decisions throughout
   - Each decision leads to different outcome
   - Teaches cause-and-effect
   - Example: "You receive a suspicious email..."
   - Output: Decision map (Appendix E template)

2. **Simulation** (for technical topics)
   - Step-by-step walkthrough of a process
   - Example: "Configure cloud security settings"
   - Interactive checklist format
   - Output: Simulation specification document

3. **Matching/Sorting** (for categorization)
   - Match concepts to definitions
   - Sort scenarios by risk level
   - Simple but effective
   - Output: Question bank document

**Interactive Content Specification** (create for each):
- Module name and objective
- Interactive type selected
- User flow diagram
- All branch/option text
- Correct/incorrect feedback
- Graphics/assets needed

**Timeline**:
- Oct 23-24: Writers draft interactive specs
- Oct 25-26: Content Lead reviews
- Oct 27-28: Writers revise
- Oct 29: Final specs approved

## Risk Management

### High-Priority Risks

**Risk 1: SME Unavailability**
- **Impact**: Content delays, reduced quality
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Mitigation**:
  - Identify backup SMEs for each category (identify by Sep 25)
  - Schedule interviews early (by Oct 10)
  - Have SMEs commit 6+ hours total
- **Contingency**: Use internal expertise + external research if SME unavailable

**Risk 2: Video Production Delays**
- **Impact**: Missing launch window
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Mitigation**:
  - Hire experienced video producer
  - Source all assets (music, footage) by Oct 29
  - Build in 1-week buffer (Week 5 allows slip to Week 6)
- **Contingency**: Reduce video count, prioritize top modules

**Risk 3: Translation Quality Issues**
- **Impact**: Poor user experience in target markets
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Mitigation**:
  - Use professional translators with compliance background
  - Regional native speakers QA all content
  - Build in 1-week buffer for revisions
- **Contingency**: Accept minor issues if they don't affect safety

**Risk 4: Content Out of Scope**
- **Impact**: Scope creep, missed deadline
- **Probability**: High
- **Mitigation**:
  - Strict content outlines approved by Oct 10
  - No new topics after Week 2
  - Enforce content freeze by Nov 1
- **Contingency**: Defer to Phase 6B if new requests arise

**Risk 5: Regional Customization Takes Too Long**
- **Impact**: Generic content, poor market fit
- **Probability**: Medium
- **Mitigation**:
  - Include regional notes in outline phase
  - Build customization into translation workflow
  - Regional managers provide examples by Oct 8
- **Contingency**: Deploy generic version first, customize post-launch

### Tracking & Escalation

**Status Tracking**:
- Weekly progress dashboard (Appendix J)
- File tracking spreadsheet (Google Sheets)
- Daily standup with writers (15 min async updates)

**Red Flag Indicators**:
- Any deliverable >3 days behind schedule
- Quality issues requiring major revisions
- SME availability dropping below 80%
- Video production timeline slipping

**Escalation Triggers**:
- Miss any week 1-6 deadline → Immediate team meeting
- >10% content missing by Nov 1 → Project Manager escalation
- Translation not started by Nov 13 → VP escalation
