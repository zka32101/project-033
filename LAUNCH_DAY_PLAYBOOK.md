- Script: "Have you checked that you're using the correct email? Try password reset."
- Escalation: If still fails after 2 attempts → tech support queue

- Script: "Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache."

**Payment Issues** (Expected: 1-2% of traffic):
- Script: "Payment processing can take a few minutes. Check your email for confirmation."

**Feature Not Working** (Expected: <1% of traffic):
- Script: "This feature is new! Try these troubleshooting steps..."

**Language/Localization Issues** (Expected: 2-3% of traffic):
- Script: Check user's language preference, offer to switch or escalate to localization team

## CONTINGENCY TRIGGERS & RESPONSES

### Contingency 1: Platform Downtime (Uptime <98%)

**Trigger**: System uptime drops below 98% at any point

**Immediate Response (0-5 minutes)**:
- [ ] Page CTO immediately
- [ ] Incident commander declared
- [ ] War room assembled (engineering, ops, product, exec)
- [ ] Begin root cause analysis

**Short-term Response (5-30 minutes)**:
- [ ] Attempt rollback to last stable version
- [ ] If rollback works: Monitor for 1 hour, then gradual ramp
- [ ] If rollback fails: Scale infrastructure, identify specific issue
- [ ] Communication: "We're experiencing technical difficulties, working on fix"

**Decision Point (30 minutes)**:
- **Can fix within 1 hour**: Proceed with fix, full transparency to users
- **Cannot fix within 1 hour**: Consider launch delay (24 hours to fix)
- **Major data loss**: Launch delay (immediate decision escalation)

---

**Trigger**: By hour 6, registration rate drops 50%+ below target (target: 500K at 6 hours, trigger: <250K)

**Immediate Analysis (hour 6+)**:
- [ ] Check marketing funnel: Where are users dropping off?
- [ ] Analyze ad performance: CTR, conversion rate by channel
- [ ] Review influencer metrics: Reach, engagement
- [ ] Check product: Onboarding experience, satisfaction
- [ ] Review competitors: Did major competitor launch?

**Response Options**:
- **If marketing issue**: Increase ad spend, activate reserve influencers, optimize landing page
- **If product issue**: Hotfix onboarding, communication: "Smooth out some early experience bugs"
- **If competitive issue**: Accelerate marketing, highlight unique features

**Escalation**:
- [ ] CEO + VP Marketing emergency meeting
- [ ] Activate reserve budget ($20K emergency ad spend)
